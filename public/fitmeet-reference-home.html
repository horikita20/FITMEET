    About FitMeet

    Fitness challenges. Fun games.One Sunday crew.

    FitMeet turns your Sunday morning into a fitness game. Every week, we meet at a new gym or outdoor spot in Patna for team challenges, fun games and good company.

    Warm up together, play as a team, grab snacks and goodies, and cheer each other on. Come solo, leave with a crew.

    What happens on your first Sunday

    Never been to a FitMeet? Here’s how the morning goes, so you know exactly what to expect before you walk in.

    1

    Walk in

    The FitMeet team welcomes you at the venue and tells you how the morning will run. You don’t need to know anyone.

    2

    Warm up together

    The whole group warms up with a coach, so you’re ready to move and never left guessing what to do.

    3

    Take on the challenges

    Strength, bodyweight, HIIT and team games. Go at your own pace; the point is to play, not to compete with the pros.

    4

    Win goodies, meet people

    Finish with prizes, snacks, high-fives and new friends. Come back next Sunday to a new place and the same crew.
