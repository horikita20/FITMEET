Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a professional runner?

No. Beginners and experienced runners are welcome. Choose a comfortable pace and take part at your own level.

How do I register?

Use any Register Now button and submit the short form. The demo prepares an email with your details for you to send.

Where is the meetup held?

The event is listed in Patna, Bihar. Confirm the exact venue with the organizer before attending.

Can a gym or brand collaborate?

Yes. Use the contact form and select the relevant collaboration option.
